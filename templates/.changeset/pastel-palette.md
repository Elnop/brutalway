template-vite takes the pastel palette: yellow `#ffe98f`, pink `#ffb8e6`, blue `#b3c5ff`, with dark text on them through a new `--on-accent` token, which replaces `--on-blue`. The focus outline uses its own `--focus` token, a strong blue that keeps its contrast on the cream page. A done task shows a dark check mark on yellow. template-fastify uses the same pastel colors in the Swagger UI theme, with dark text on the blue method badge.
