The vite projects take a pastel palette in every styling value: yellow `#ffe98f`, pink `#ffb8e6` and blue `#b3c5ff`. Text on these colors uses a new `on-accent` token, dark in both themes, which replaces `on-blue`, and the focus outline has its own `focus` token so it keeps a strong blue on the cream page. With `dark-theme`, the accents stay pastel in the dark and keep their dark text. With shadcn/ui, the primary and destructive buttons and the checked checkbox use `on-accent` too.
